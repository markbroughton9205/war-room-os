# EARTH KNOWLEDGE SOURCE REGISTRY
## War Room OS — Global Knowledge Infrastructure Discovery (Verified 2026)

**Mission:** A verified, machine-accessible registry of Earth's knowledge infrastructure across all major domains — software, security, medicine, biology, earth science, human history, government, economics, science, geography, and general knowledge — searched GLOBALLY, with deliberate coverage of Africa, Asia, Latin America, the Middle East, and Oceania, including local-language sources.

**Verification standard:** Every source was checked against official documentation and, wherever possible, live-probed during August 2026. No source, endpoint, credential, or signup URL was invented. Sources confirmed dead or degraded are marked **DISCONTINUED** / **DEGRADED** with their replacement where one exists. An ordinary website is never called an API — search-only resources are labeled `SEARCH INTERFACE`.

**Companion registry:** African-focused sources live in the separate verified registry `war_room_africa_api_registry.md` (~110 sources). This registry cross-references rather than duplicates them.

## AUTHORITY TIER LEGEND

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A** | Official government / scientific primary source (NIST, WHO, USGS, national registries) |
| **B** | Major academic institution or international organization (EMBL-EBI, Cornell, OECD) |
| **C** | Established nonprofit / open-data institution (GBIF, iNaturalist, Wikimedia) |
| **D** | Commercial provider (VirusTotal, Shodan, DrugBank) |
| **E** | Community / open-source project (GloBI, Rosetta Code) |

## ACCESS TYPE VOCABULARY

`REST` · `GRAPHQL` · `SPARQL` · `OAI-PMH` · `IIIF` · `SDMX` · `ODATA` · `STAC` · `TAP` · `WMS/WFS` · `PXWEB` · `BULK DOWNLOAD` · `FEED` (RSS/Atom/CSAF/MISP) · `REPOSITORY` (git) · `SEARCH INTERFACE` (no machine API — negative finding) · `COMMERCIAL-GATED`

## EVIDENCE CLASSIFICATIONS (preserved from sources — never flatten)

`TRAD` recorded traditional use / pharmacopoeia record (NOT clinical proof) · `EXP` experimental/literature evidence · `PRED` computational prediction · `NOMEN` nomenclatural only · `CLIN` clinical · Threat-intel distinctions (confirmed-exploited vs predicted vs community-reported) are likewise preserved per source.
