# War Room OS — Earth Knowledge Base: Execution Plan

## Goal
Verified global registry of machine-accessible knowledge infrastructure across 11 domains → organized into the 25 required registry categories + MASTER REGISTRY + sections A–Z. 34 verification fields per source. No invented sources; verify 2026 operational status; mark DISCONTINUED + replacement.

## Strategy
Two waves of 8 background subagents (max parallel), custom role `earth_kb_hunter` (34-field schema, access-type precision, global-geography mandate, no US-default). Reuse prior Africa registry knowledge where overlapping (history/archives/stats — agents told to avoid re-reporting African sources already registered; focus global).

### Wave 1 (8 agents)
1. Software & coding (registries, GitHub/GitLab APIs, Stack Exchange, standards orgs, docs)
2. Bugs/patches/software history (OSV, deps.dev, endoflife.date, issue trackers, vendor advisories)
3. Cybersecurity (NVD, CVE.org, CISA KEV, ATT&CK, abuse.ch, OTX, VT, CIRCL, national CERTs, EPSS)
4. Medical knowledge & diseases (PubMed/E-utilities, ClinicalTrials, ICD API, Orphanet, MedlinePlus, UMLS, GARD)
5. Pharmaceuticals (openFDA, RxNorm, DailyMed, PubChem, ChEMBL, EMA, WHO ATC, DrugCentral)
6. Traditional/indigenous medicine + ethnopharmacology (TCM DBs, IMPPAT, MPNS, PROTA, ethnobotany DBs)
7. Biology & biodiversity (GBIF, COL, WoRMS, IUCN, NCBI Taxonomy, BOLD, iNaturalist, POWO)
8. Genomics & molecular bio (NCBI, EBI/Ensembl, UniProt, InterPro, PDB, KEGG status, STRING)

### Wave 2 (8 agents)
9. Human origins/archaeology + world history (Seshat, Pleiades, Europeana, global primary sources, newspapers archives global)
10. Government/law/economics (legislation APIs global, EUR-Lex, sanctions, OpenCorporates, GLEIF, elections, budgets)
11. Science & engineering + patents (arXiv, INSPIRE, ADS, Materials Project, NOMAD, OEIS, PDG, EPO OPS, USPTO, WIPO)
12. Maps/GIS/satellite + space/astronomy (Copernicus Data Space, USGS, Open-Meteo, NASA APIs, JPL SSD, SIMBAD, Gaia)
13. Academic research + archives/libraries/museums (global: OpenAlex etc. + Trove, Delpher, NDL, DDB, Gallica-global, aggregators)
14. Statistics/census/demographics + international orgs (OECD, Eurostat, UN SDMX, e-Stat Japan, IBGE, StatsCan, MOSPI, UNHCR, IOM)
15. General web/reference knowledge (Wikimedia suite, Common Crawl, dictionaries, Stack Exchange, encyclopedias, RSS/feed infra)
16. Geographic coverage audit + non-Western/regional sources (CNKI/J-STAGE/SciELO/Redalyc/CyberLeninka/KISTI/Indian/Arabic/LatAm/African already-done cross-ref) + rare/specialized (registry 25)

### Stage 3 — Integration
Merge, dedupe, geographic coverage audit (9 regions), authority tiers A–E, 25-category registry, master table, sections A–Z. Output: /mnt/agents/output/earth_knowledge_source_registry.md

## Rules (from brief)
34 verification fields per entry; never call a website an API; preserve evidence classifications (esp. traditional medicine & threat intel); geographic audit mandatory; Tier A–E authority labeling.
