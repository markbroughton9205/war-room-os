Tiers: A=official gov/intl primary, B=major academic/intl org, C=established nonprofit/open-data, D=commercial, E=community.
Cross-referenced (registered elsewhere, not re-listed): UN Comtrade, IMF, World Bank, OECD SDMX, V-Dem, Afrobarometer/IIAG (Africa registry).
