## Completion Standard Used

A domain was upgraded only when:

1. Its core requested subdomains have authoritative sources.
2. More than one geography/institutional perspective is represented.
3. At least one operational access path exists for major functions: structured data, stable documentation, searchable archives, or documented labs/training.
4. Access, licensing and deprecation constraints are explicit.
5. Remaining deficiencies are depth/localization issues rather than absence of the domain itself.

## Duplicate / Overlap Audit

The prior War Room source inventory was **not supplied inside this mission artifact**. Therefore the research does not guess which source duplicates an existing War Room provider. Major records default to **UNVERIFIED_RELATIONSHIP**, except explicit negative/deprecation findings. The reconciliation pass should compare stable organization + dataset/product + canonical URL + identifiers and must not collapse distinct datasets merely because they belong to the same organization.

## Cross-Domain Access Blockers

- **Rights:** archives, standards and evidence databases can restrict redistribution/TDM.
- **Credentials:** some interfaces require accounts, keys, partnership, approval or licences.
- **Format:** PDFs, images and handwritten manuscripts are not structured data.
- **Versioning:** medicine, software docs, laws and APIs change.
- **Jurisdiction:** global medicine/finance sources cannot replace current local requirements.
- **Cultural governance:** Indigenous/traditional materials can have authority, consent and sovereignty constraints beyond copyright.
