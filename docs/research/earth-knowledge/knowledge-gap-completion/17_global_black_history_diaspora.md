# 17_global_black_history_diaspora.md

**War Room OS — Global Knowledge-Gap Completion**  
**Verification date:** 2026-08-13  
**Previous status:** PARTIAL  
**Recommended status:** **PRESENT**

## Executive Summary

The domain now has a defensible global backbone rather than a U.S.- or Atlantic-only reading list. UNESCO's completed General History of Africa provides an Africa-wide chronology and an explicit **Africa-and-its-diasporas / Global Africa** spine [BH01]. SlaveVoyages and Enslaved.org provide downloadable/machine-readable slavery infrastructure [BH03–BH04]. SSDA, dLOC, Schomburg, the Library of Congress, British Library EAP, Qatar Digital Library, Brazil's national digital library, South African/French archives and HMML's Timbuktu work supply primary-source depth [BH05–BH15].

This supports **PRESENT at the knowledge-infrastructure level**. It does not mean every national/local archive is covered. The biggest remaining weaknesses are Asia/Oceania diaspora depth, uneven African/local digitization, language/OCR limitations and the structural bias of colonial/state archives.

## Scope

Coverage includes African prehistory, civilizations, kingdoms/empires, archaeology, manuscripts, oral history, trade, philosophy/religion/science/medicine, military/art/literature, colonialism/resistance/independence/post-colonial history; trans-Atlantic, Indian Ocean, intra-American and related slave systems; abolition/emancipation/reconstruction/migration/exile/return; maroon/rebellion/civil-rights/Pan-African/labor/women/military/diplomatic/intellectual histories; and cultural fields including music, religion, literature, oral tradition, art, architecture, fashion, food, dance, folklore, philosophy, languages, education, science and medicine.
